# Lewis Kelly SMC Trading Strategy

Tactical Smart Money Concepts (SMC) trend-following and reversal workflow synthesized from Lewis Kelly's verified methodology.

## Strategy Summary
- **Source Platform:** YouTube (`@Lewiskelly8`)
- **Primary Market:** Forex, Indices, and Futures.
- **Core Timeframe Pairs:**
  - Aggressive Day Trading: **M15** (Higher Timeframe Trend) + **M1** (Lower Timeframe Entry).
  - Swing Trading: **H1 / M5** or **H4 / M15**.
- **Edge:** Entering on M1 Change of Character (CHoCH) inside an M15 key Area of Interest (AOI), aligning early with the higher-timeframe continuation for asymmetric **1:5+ Risk-to-Reward (RR)**.

---

## 1. Market Structure & Trend Definition (Higher Timeframe - M15)
1. **Trend Identification:** Determine whether price is delivering bullish or bearish order flow:
   - **Bearish Trend:** Series of lower highs (LH) followed by lower lows (LL) and confirmed Break of Structure (BOS).
   - **Bullish Trend:** Series of higher lows (HL) followed by higher highs (HH).
2. **Trading Range:** Define the operational range between the active swing high and swing low. Trade strictly in the direction of this higher timeframe structure.

---

## 2. Retracement Phase (M1 Pullback)
- Once the M15 puts in a new lower low, price begins a counter-trend retracement.
- On the M1 timeframe, the internal order flow will appear temporarily opposite to the M15 trend (e.g. M1 is temporarily bullish during an M15 bearish pullback).
- **Wait for Key Area of Interest (AOI):** Do not front-run the pullback; wait until price reaches the M15 supply/demand or liquidity zone.

---

## 3. Entry Trigger: Lower Timeframe Reversal (M1 Change of Character)
- **Change of Character (CHoCH):** Wait for the M1 timeframe to shift from counter-trend to trend-alignment (e.g. M1 breaks its last higher low and flips bearish at the M15 supply level).
- **Execution:** Enter on the confirmed M1 shift:
  - **Stop Loss:** Placed tightly above the M1 reversal swing point.
  - **Take Profit Target:** Placed at the previous M15 structural low/high, expecting full trend continuation.
- **Risk:Reward:** Typically delivers 1:3 to 1:5+ RR due to minimal M1 invalidation distance.

---

## 4. Invalidation & Risk Management
- If the M1 structural high breaches the M15 invalidation level, the thesis is void—cut immediately.
- Never force trades outside the aligned timeframe pairing (e.g. mixing M5 entry on an H4 zone leads to timing mismatch).
