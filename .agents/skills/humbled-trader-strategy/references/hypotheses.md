# Mined Hypotheses for Humbled Trader (youtube)

Empirical rules and observations extracted from content:

### [LONG] Overnight Multi-Day Continuation for Trend Longs
- **Category:** `holding_period`
- **Timestamp:** `02:46 - 03:06`
- **Falsifiable Statement:** Trend-following day-trade longs frequently demonstrate momentum continuation into the subsequent session; transitioning successful intraday momentum longs into overnight swing trades yields superior total performance compared to closing them on the same day.
- **Setup Criteria:** High ADR stocks, high volume/liquidity, daily chart trend-following breakout, sector momentum
- **Holding Period:** Overnight / 2-day swing trade
- **Statistical Claim:** Overnight and swing trades generally outperformed intraday same-day longs in verified P&L analysis
- **Verbatim Ground Truth:**
> "And this is where the analysis became really useful. My overnight and swing trades generally performed better than my same-day longs. And part of the reason is that my day trading strategy that I love the most, the trend following long, a lot of times I see continuation and strength into the second day. Hence, that's when the day trade becomes a much stronger overnight"

### [SHORT] Intraday Close for Short Selling (Avoid Overnight Short Swings)
- **Category:** `holding_period`
- **Timestamp:** `03:08 - 03:16`
- **Falsifiable Statement:** Short selling positions deteriorate over multi-day periods due to upward market drift and overnight gap risks; short setups must be executed strictly as intraday same-day trades and never held overnight as swing trades.
- **Setup Criteria:** Intraday momentum exhaustion, high ADR equities
- **Holding Period:** Strictly same-day intraday (close before market close)
- **Statistical Claim:** Same-day shorts significantly outperformed multi-day short swings
- **Verbatim Ground Truth:**
> "swing trade. Another thing I found is that I found the same-day trading shorts performed much better than swing shorts. So that's a sign that I need to avoid short selling as a swing trade. And when"

- **Category:** `stock_selection`
- **Timestamp:** `03:21 - 03:45`
- **Falsifiable Statement:** Sustainable trading edge requires sufficient price range expansion; traders should filter for stocks exhibiting ADR > 4% with strong liquidity. Equities with ADR < 3-4% lack the range necessary to compensate for trade execution costs and risk.

- **Holding Period:** Intraday to multi-day swing
- **Statistical Claim:** Strongest historical trading results concentrated exclusively in high ADR stocks; flat/negative on low ADR
- **Verbatim Ground Truth:**
> "that some of my strongest results generally come from really high ADR stocks with really strong liquidity. ADR stands for average daily range, and it's a percentage. So a stock that moves a lot in a lot of range, you want to look for anything above 4% ADR or even higher. I think anything less than 4% ADR or less than 3%, that means a stock, no matter the price, it's moving in really, really small range. So as you"

So, when I grouped the trades that matched those conditions, my focus group had a 72% historical winning rate. ### [BOTH] Exponential Bet Sizing on High-Conviction A+ Setups
- **Category:** `risk_management`
- **Timestamp:** `09:06 - 09:35`
- **Falsifiable Statement:** Uniform position sizing across all trades destroys profitability by treating high-probability and low-probability setups equally; capital allocation must be exponentially skewed toward A+ setups that meet all focus criteria, while sizing down drastically or abstaining from marginal setups.
- **Setup Criteria:** Confirmed A+ setup matching full focus filter checklist (rare occurrence, non-daily)
- **Holding Period:** Flexible
- **Statistical Claim:** Allocating equal size to best and worst trades was the primary leak in trading performance
- **Verbatim Ground Truth:**
> "warrants more size, less size, or no trade at all. There's a really fancy term called exponential bet sizing. It really just means to size into your best A+ setups when they appear and trade in much smaller on the average setups and everything else. Naturally, that also means the best of the best setups for you should not appear every single day. If every trade looks like an A+ setup, then you probably need to fix your grading systems or your analysis. Remember, you don't have to trade every day, every week, or even every month."
