### [BOTH] In-Play Asset Filter: Volume, Volatility & Catalyst Discovery
- **Category:** `stock_selection`
- **Instrument:** `equities`
- **Falsifiable Statement:** Trading technical levels only works on in-play products undergoing active price discovery, heightened volatility, and emotional order flow driven by major catalysts; trading levels on quiet, efficient instruments yields random market friction.
- **Setup Criteria:** Unusual volume, elevated ATR/volatility, major catalyst or exceptional technical setup (e.g. SpaceX IPO, metals breakouts, oil geopolitical moves)
- **Holding Period:** Flexible
- **Statistical Evidence:** In-play products concentrate retail and institutional emotion, allowing predictable technical reactions
- **Invalidation Risk:** Fading volume and declining volatility return the asset to efficient random walk
- **Verbatim Ground Truth:**
> "I only trade inplay stocks or products. An inplay stock is one experiencing unusual volume, volatility, a major catalyst, or an exceptional technical setup that has attracted significant attention from traders... Most levels are noise. You are better off flipping a coin. But when a stock has increased tension, emotion, volume, and volatility, you have the conditions where these technical levels become useful."

### [BOTH] Proper Consolidation & Tension Building Before Breakout
- **Category:** `execution`
- **Instrument:** `equities`
- **Falsifiable Statement:** High-probability breakouts require tight pre-break consolidation where price refuses to pull back; this consolidation builds structural tension where shorts position for a drop and longs take profits, creating an explosive liquidity vacuum when breached.
- **Setup Criteria:** Shallow retracement after large move, price equilibrium holding tight beneath resistance or above support
- **Holding Period:** Breakout expansion duration
- **Statistical Evidence:** Tight consolidation before level breach prevents false breakouts and drives immediate runaway expansion
- **Invalidation Risk:** Deep, loose retracement indicates lack of institutional absorption
- **Verbatim Ground Truth:**
> "Consolidation is price acceptance and you need these periods of relative equilibrium to gauge just how strong the buying or selling is after a large move in one direction... If we consolidate and don't pull back, when that break higher finally happens, all the longs need to re-enter and the shorts need to cover. This is easily one of the most underestimated points in trading breakouts."

### [BOTH] Multi-Timeframe Level Alignment for Institutional Swarming
- **Category:** `edge`
- **Instrument:** `equities`
- **Falsifiable Statement:** Technical levels gain exponential reliability when intraday inflection points align with major levels on the daily and weekly charts, uniting short-term day traders, swing traders, and institutions on the same side of the order book.
- **Setup Criteria:** Intraday (2-min / 5-min) level coinciding exactly with prior day high/low or multi-week chart pivot
- **Holding Period:** Flexible
- **Statistical Evidence:** Multi-timeframe confluence generates instantaneous volume surges and rapid price displacement
- **Invalidation Risk:** Level visible only on a single low timeframe lacks broader market participation
- **Verbatim Ground Truth:**
> "Support and resistance exist everywhere... But a level becomes especially interesting when several of those charts point toward the same price. The more time frames that align, the more market participants you get on your side... you want everyone swarming to be on the same side of the market."
