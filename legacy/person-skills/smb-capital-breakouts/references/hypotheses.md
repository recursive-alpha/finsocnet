### [BOTH] 9/10 Technical Chart Level as Independent Catalyst
- **Category:** `stock_selection`
- **Instrument:** `equities`
- **Falsifiable Statement:** A technical breakout does not always require breaking news headlines; when an instrument breaks a multi-week or multi-month resistance level (graded 9/10 or 10/10), the clearing of that technical level itself serves as the market catalyst.
- **Setup Criteria:** Multi-week or multi-month consolidation on daily chart, clean horizontal resistance graded >= 9/10
- **Holding Period:** Intraday momentum to multi-day swing
- **Statistical Evidence:** Taking only 9/10 and 10/10 levels eliminates 80% of amateur chop and creates asymmetric desk profitability
- **Invalidation Risk:** Trading 4/10 to 6/10 levels inside sloppy ranges produces paper cuts and flat/negative P&L
- **Verbatim Ground Truth:**
> "A breakout trade doesn't always need breaking news. When a stock clears a level it couldn't for weeks or months, that level itself is the catalyst... When Vince presented this daily chart to me, I asked him straight, 'Scale of 1 to 10, how important is that level?' He said a nine, and he was dead right."

### [BOTH] Pre-Breakout Relative Volume (RVOL >= 2x) Participation Filter
- **Category:** `stock_selection`
- **Instrument:** `equities`
- **Falsifiable Statement:** News brings retail attention, but elevated Relative Volume (RVOL >= 2.0x-3.0x) provides institutional confirmation; breakouts attempted on sub-normal volume are traps and must be rejected.
- **Setup Criteria:** Full day RVOL running at least 2x normal participation leading into the breakout session
- **Holding Period:** Flexible
- **Statistical Evidence:** Breakouts with RVOL > 2x exhibit high follow-through; breakouts below 1x fail over 70% of the time
- **Invalidation Risk:** Low volume on breakout approach indicates lack of institutional sponsorship
- **Verbatim Ground Truth:**
> "News brings attention, but volume brings confirmation. Even without a headline, you need to see elevated ARVO, relative volume, confirming that big money participants are actively involved in the name... If a stock is trying to clear a monthly ceiling on half of the volume it normally does, it's a trap. If it's trading on two times or three times volume, real order flow is entering the arena."

### [LONG] Tape Reading Aggression & Frictionless Offer Clearing
- **Category:** `execution`
- **Instrument:** `equities`
- **Falsifiable Statement:** Genuine institutional breakouts reveal distinct tape character: aggressive lifting of offers, offers clearing without friction, remarkably shallow pullbacks with declining volume, and rapid upward velocity.
- **Setup Criteria:** Level breached, trades executing on offer, bids stepping up, shallow retracements
- **Holding Period:** Intraday momentum
- **Statistical Evidence:** Aggressive tape lifting distinguishes high-velocity breakouts from false head-fakes
- **Invalidation Risk:** Offers reload heavily or bids soften upon level breach
- **Verbatim Ground Truth:**
> "When ETHA took out 1912, what did the tape look like? One, they were aggressively paying the offer. Two, the offers cleared without friction... Three, intraday pullbacks were remarkably shallow... Four, pullbacks were shallow and with decreasing volume. Five, the stock went up higher and faster than anyone expected."

### [BOTH] Non-Round Stop Loss Placement Rule (.09 / .14 / .24 Offset)
- **Category:** `risk_management`
- **Instrument:** `equities`
- **Falsifiable Statement:** Setting stop losses directly on obvious round numbers (.00, .10, .25, .50) leaves orders vulnerable to market maker liquidity sweeps; offsetting stops by 1 cent (e.g. .09, .14, .24) substantially improves trade survival rates.
- **Setup Criteria:** Stop placed strictly below breakout candle low or pre-break base with 1-cent offset
- **Holding Period:** Trade duration
- **Statistical Evidence:** One extra cent offset dramatically eliminates false stop-outs from predatory sweeps
- **Invalidation Risk:** Placing stops on round numbers exposes trades to unnecessary liquidity hunt losses
- **Verbatim Ground Truth:**
> "Your stop goes strictly below the low of the breakout candle, or the immediate base before the break... A quick pro tip on stops, never set stops right on round numbers like 10 cents, or 20 cents, or 25 cents. Give it that extra penny, use 9 cents, or 14 cents, or 24 cents. That one penny dramatically improves your win rate against random liquidity sweeps."

### [LONG] Adding to Winners on Intraday Consolidation Wick Breaks
- **Category:** `execution`
- **Instrument:** `equities`
- **Falsifiable Statement:** When a breakout demonstrates follow-through without pulling back, traders should pyramid capital by identifying 1-minute consolidation pauses, placing buy stops above wick highs, and trailing stops to the consolidation low.
- **Setup Criteria:** Initial position in profit, 1-minute consolidation flag forming, stop trailed to flag low
- **Holding Period:** Momentum expansion
- **Statistical Evidence:** Adding to winning momentum trades multiplies total R-multiple return without increasing baseline risk
- **Invalidation Risk:** Breakdown below the 1-minute consolidation base invalidates the add
- **Verbatim Ground Truth:**
> "When the trade didn't go down and held higher, he didn't just sit on his hands. He identified consolidation pauses on the 1-minute tape, placed buy stops just above the wick highs where sellers temporarily stepped in, and added into the new push while trailing his risk up to the low of consolidation."

### [BOTH] The Scratch Rule: Immediate Liquidation on Momentum Stall
- **Category:** `holding_period`
- **Instrument:** `equities`
- **Falsifiable Statement:** Momentum breakout trades must work immediately; if price breaks a level but velocity stalls, bids soften, and the tape dies, the position must be dumped immediately at breakeven or a nominal 1-2 cent loss rather than waiting for a full stop loss.
- **Setup Criteria:** Level breached but upward velocity halts within 1-2 minutes, volume dies
- **Holding Period:** Instant exit (< 2 minutes)
- **Statistical Evidence:** Scratching dead trades preserves risk capital for genuine momentum runners
- **Invalidation Risk:** Passively holding a stalled trade turns scratches into full painful losses
- **Verbatim Ground Truth:**
> "To trade this setup with real size, you must learn how to scratch. If you pay a new high expecting instant momentum and the velocity simply isn't there, the bid soften and the tape dies, you dump it. You cut it. You get flat immediately at breakeven or two-cent loss. Don't sit there passively taking a full stop if the reason you took the trade disappeared."
