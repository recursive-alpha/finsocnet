# Verified Empirical Hypotheses & Rulebook: Tim Furey (Tim Furey (Trade View))

Grounded trading hypotheses and execution principles extracted from source material:

### [BOTH] Defensive Execution First: Profitability Arises from Loss Prevention
- **Category:** `risk_management`
- **Instrument:** `futures`
- **Falsifiable Statement:** Long-term market survival and profitability stem entirely from defense and loss minimization rather than offensive prediction; traders must master how not to lose money before capital can compound.
- **Setup Criteria:** Applied unconditionally across all trades and market regimes
- **Holding Period:** Flexible
- **Statistical Evidence:** Floor market makers generated $28.5K+ monthly profits strictly by limiting losses to 5-10 ticks with zero emphasis on offensive gimmicks
- **Invalidation Risk:** Focusing on making money rather than risk preservation leads to rapid account ruin
- **Verbatim Ground Truth:**
> "What is the biggest thing I learned in the trading pit? And how did we make money? And to answer that is you had to learn how to lose... That's the only thing they taught you is defense. They didn't teach you anything about how to make money. They taught you everything on how not to lose money... That's how you make money is by not losing it."

### [BOTH] The Power of the Lean: Entering Only with Structural Order Flow Support
- **Category:** `execution`
- **Instrument:** `futures`
- **Falsifiable Statement:** Entering trades without an immediate order flow backstop creates severe adverse risk; traders should enter only at prices where they have 'the lean'—structural support allowing an immediate exit at breakeven if the market stalls.
- **Setup Criteria:** Bid/Offer structural lean, immediate offer placed upon fill to capture spread or scratch
- **Holding Period:** Scalp to intraday
- **Statistical Evidence:** Eliminates catastrophic slippage by anchoring entries to liquidity pools
- **Invalidation Risk:** Market trades through the lean without filling exit orders
- **Verbatim Ground Truth:**
> "It's called the power of the lean. If I'm at 50 bid, if I get in at 50, I'm offering at 55 right away. Or if the market seems like it's going to turn, boom, I break even. It's the power to lean. I know I can get out. If I get in at 50 and someone hits me, I know I can get out at 50. If it goes up, great. If it goes down, I better be out."

### [BOTH] Strict 5-to-10 Tick Stop Loss Discipline & Mandatory Account Suspension
- **Category:** `risk_management`
- **Instrument:** `futures`
- **Falsifiable Statement:** Losses must be clipped within a narrow predefined boundary (5 to 10 ticks max); exceeding 10 ticks indicates complete breakdown of process and warrants immediate trading suspension.
- **Setup Criteria:** Stop loss triggered at -5 to -10 ticks
- **Holding Period:** Instant exit
- **Statistical Evidence:** Zero tolerance for losses exceeding 10 ticks preserves trading firm capital across decades
- **Invalidation Risk:** Tolerating open drawdown beyond 10 ticks risks fatal account damage
- **Verbatim Ground Truth:**
> "You're only allowed to lose five ticks... they never question it. If you broke even, they never question it. But if you did 10 ticks, they're like 'Okay, what happened here?' If it was over 10 ticks, forget about it... they would suspend you for losing more than five ticks if you didn't have a good explanation."

### [LONG] Squint-Test Relative Strength Trend Following: Never Fade Momentum
- **Category:** `stock_selection`
- **Instrument:** `equities`
- **Falsifiable Statement:** Attempting to short parabolic or trending bull markets because they appear overvalued is a chronic loser trap; traders must apply the 'squint test' to follow prevailing trend momentum where the strong gets stronger and the weak gets weaker.
- **Setup Criteria:** Higher highs and higher lows on daily chart, strong relative strength against market
- **Holding Period:** Trend duration
- **Statistical Evidence:** Fading high-momentum runners (e.g. Yahoo, Tesla) accounts for the vast majority of retail liquidations
- **Invalidation Risk:** Shorting a rising trend without structural breakdown
- **Verbatim Ground Truth:**
> "Take a chart, print it out, put it on the floor, stand up on your chair, squint your eyes. What direction is it going? Up or down? Okay, it's going up. Why would you ever short this? To be successful in trading, it's relative strength, relative weakness... The strong gets stronger and the weak get weaker... Trade the trend. The trend is your friend."

### [LONG] Escalator Up, Elevator Down: Buying Sharp Pullbacks in Healthy Uptrends
- **Category:** `execution`
- **Instrument:** `equities`
- **Falsifiable Statement:** Bull markets grind slowly higher (escalator up) and experience sharp, sudden pullbacks (elevator down); rather than shorting the elevator, disciplined trend traders use sudden dips in healthy uptrends as prime low-risk buying opportunities.
- **Setup Criteria:** Strong uptrend, sharp panic pullback into key moving average or support
- **Holding Period:** Swing
- **Statistical Evidence:** Buying sharp pullbacks in leading uptrends produces rapid V-rebound profits
- **Invalidation Risk:** Break of macro structural support invalidates the uptrend
- **Verbatim Ground Truth:**
> "Escalator up, it's slow. Elevator down, right? Sometimes you got to buy on the elevator down where everybody wants to try to make the elevator down and it doesn't always happen... If you're following the trend, if it goes down, you look for your points to buy if it's trending up, right? And if you get sharp sell-offs, you got to look to buy because that is a healthy stock or healthy market."

### [BOTH] The Ability to Walk Away: Non-Trading as the Ultimate Defensive Edge
- **Category:** `holding_period`
- **Instrument:** `equities`
- **Falsifiable Statement:** When market conditions lack clarity or edge, the most profitable decision is walking away entirely; preserving capital by not trading for days or weeks positions traders to strike with full force when obvious edge returns.
- **Setup Criteria:** Chop, conflicting signals, or lack of structural lean
- **Holding Period:** Days to weeks of non-trading
- **Statistical Evidence:** Eliminates overtrading churn and preserves emotional stamina
- **Invalidation Risk:** Trading out of boredom degrades capital
- **Verbatim Ground Truth:**
> "Now the most important thing is the defense and the ability to walk away, right? Not to trade—that could be your best trade is not trading, to walk away. Maybe it's for a day, maybe it's for a week, maybe you just sit back and watch. But that one day your gut's going to be like 'this is the trade', you put it on."
