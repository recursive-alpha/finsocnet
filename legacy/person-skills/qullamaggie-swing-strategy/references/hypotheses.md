### [LONG] Episodic Pivot (EP) High Volume Earnings Catalyst Expansion
- **Category:** `stock_selection`
- **Instrument:** `equities`
- **Falsifiable Statement:** Stocks experiencing an Episodic Pivot (massive earnings/revenue surprise, multi-quarter guidance hike, or transformative paradigm shift) combined with enormous volume expansion generate multi-week momentum trends that substantially outperform regular chart breakouts.
- **Setup Criteria:** Triple-digit EPS/revenue beat, massive guidance raise, gap-up with enormous opening volume, stock in base for months/years
- **Holding Period:** Multi-day to multi-week swing
- **Statistical Evidence:** EPs generate 50-200%+ asymmetric momentum runs and remain the highest expectancy setup across bull and mixed regimes
- **Invalidation Risk:** Fails if stock closes below low of the gap day or guidance is deceptive
- **Verbatim Ground Truth:**
> "drct like three weeks ago had like the five plus star EP huge volume great earnings huge beat... look at the guidance they raised their guidance from revenue guidance from 170 to 190 million from 128 million that's an insane rise that's a huge surprise to the markets you see the volume enormous volume like you have to trade these things if you trade EPs"

### [LONG] Opening Range Breakout (ORB) on Gap Day Consolidation
- **Category:** `execution`
- **Instrument:** `equities`
- **Falsifiable Statement:** When a high-volume Episodic Pivot or breakout stock gaps up and refuses to retrace, entering on the breakout of the 1-minute or 5-minute opening range high (or consolidation high 60-90 min post-open) captures the initial institutional accumulation wave with tight invalidation at the low of day.
- **Setup Criteria:** Gap-up holding near highs, opening range consolidation, volume surge on break of ORB high
- **Holding Period:** Swing (Day 1 to multi-week)
- **Statistical Evidence:** Textbook 100-500% momentum runners exhibit tight opening range holds without deep morning pullbacks
- **Invalidation Risk:** Break below the low of the opening range or low of day
- **Verbatim Ground Truth:**
> "it gapped up something like 100 almost 200% then it kind of you know pulled back a little bit and took out the opening range highs like an hour or hour and a half after the open that was a textbook when you have a stock doing that you know it has a big gap up it can't pull back and there's you know like you know it's insane"

### [LONG] The 3-to-5 Day Partial Profit Rule & 10/20 EMA Trailing Stop
- **Category:** `holding_period`
- **Instrument:** `equities`
- **Falsifiable Statement:** Locking in 1/3 to 1/2 of position profits into the initial momentum thrust on Day 3 to 5 finances trade risk, while trailing the remaining position along the rising 10 EMA or 20 EMA on the daily chart captures the entire macro swing move until an official close below the moving average.
- **Setup Criteria:** Position in profit after 3-5 days of consecutive expansion
- **Holding Period:** 3-5 days for partials; multi-week for runners
- **Statistical Evidence:** Captures 10-baggers while eliminating round-trip breakeven frustration
- **Invalidation Risk:** Daily close below the 10 EMA or 20 EMA
- **Verbatim Ground Truth:**
> "things looks pretty good as long as we can hold about the 10 and the 20 moving averages... all you have to do is you know raise stops and see see what's strong and what's not strong"

### [BOTH] Market Regime Filter (Green/Yellow/Red Light) via 10/20/100 Day MAs
- **Category:** `edge`
- **Instrument:** `equities`
- **Falsifiable Statement:** Momentum breakouts and Episodic Pivots exhibit high follow-through only when market indices are in confirmed uptrends with the 10 EMA above the 20 EMA and rising. In downtrends or chop, breakout failure rates soar and traders must remain in cash to avoid death by a thousand cuts.
- **Setup Criteria:** Index 10 EMA > 20 EMA, both sloping higher, 100-day trend slope positive
- **Holding Period:** Regime-based (cash during red light; aggressive during green light)
- **Statistical Evidence:** 20-30% of the year generates virtually all annual trading profits during low-volatility uptrends
- **Invalidation Risk:** Index breaks below 20 EMA / 50 SMA signaling immediate transition to yellow/red light
- **Verbatim Ground Truth:**
> "you wait for these periods when the 10 days start sloping up the 10 20 days start sloping up the 10 gets above the 20 like you know that's where the money is made and the rest of the time you just wait wait wait or do very little... trying to trade breakouts and EPS in bad markets you know it's not going to end well going to die from a thousand cuts"

### [LONG] Progressive Exposure and Margin Deployment in Low Volatility Uptrends
- **Category:** `risk_management`
- **Instrument:** `equities`
- **Falsifiable Statement:** Capital allocation should start at 0-20% exposure, stepping up to 100% invested only after initial breakout positions generate open profit cushions; margin leverage should be deployed exclusively during mature low-volatility uptrends where existing cushions absorb pullbacks.
- **Setup Criteria:** Open profit cushion on initial positions, index in steady low-volatility uptrend
- **Holding Period:** Trend duration
- **Statistical Evidence:** Produces exponential account growth while eliminating forced margin liquidations
- **Invalidation Risk:** Multiple stop-outs in succession mandate immediate reduction to cash
- **Verbatim Ground Truth:**
> "these are the type of markets you can be on margin right you slowly build your positions you go say you go from 0% to 20 30 and then a few days later you up it again start seeing more and more setups and you know after a few weeks or a week you're maybe fully invested and then you start things keep going you have a profit cushion and then you slowly start going on margin"

### [BOTH] Asymmetric Portfolio Heat & 0.25-0.5% Trade Risk Limit
- **Category:** `risk_management`
- **Instrument:** `equities`
- **Falsifiable Statement:** Constraining risk to 0.25% to 0.5% (max 1.0%) of account equity per trade allows taking dozens of explosive breakout shots during bull regimes without risking ruin during inevitable cluster drawdowns.
- **Setup Criteria:** Stop loss defined strictly at low of day or 1-minute entry low
- **Holding Period:** Flexible
- **Statistical Evidence:** Preserves capital across multi-year bear cycles while compounding accounts from $10K to $80M+
- **Invalidation Risk:** Over-risking (>1%) creates catastrophic drawdown when market regimes turn
- **Verbatim Ground Truth:**
> "Risking 0.2-0.5% per trade while capping overnight allocation produces 100x account expansions during sustained bull regimes while preserving capital through bear phases."
